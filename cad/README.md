# cad/

3D-printable brackets and enclosures. **Currently placeholder.**

Planned contents, per `../ROADMAP.md` and the engineering report:

- A small printed speaker enclosure for the I²S amplifier/speaker
  (Phase 3) — a loose speaker inside the chassis sounds weak and may
  rattle per `../docs/architecture.md` §5.6 discussion.
- Mounting brackets for the Pi, HDMI-to-CSI capture bridge, and I/O board
  (Phase 5 integration).
- Cable-routing guides.

Source files (e.g. `.f3d`, `.step`) and exported `.stl` go here once
designed; no files exist yet. Do not print/order anything against a
bracket design here until it's been checked against the as-built
component dimensions, not assumed ones.
